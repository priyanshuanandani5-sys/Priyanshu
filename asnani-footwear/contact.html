Good to Know

Common Questions

How do I place an order?

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 8408821319 with the style you like and your size. Our team will confirm availability, price and delivery with you directly.

Do you ship outside Nagpur?

Yes. Shipping is available across India. Share your city or PIN code on WhatsApp and we'll confirm the shipping details for your order.

Can I check a size before visiting the store?

Yes. Send us the style and your size on WhatsApp and we'll let you know what's in stock before you make the trip.

I saw a style on Instagram. Can I order it?

Send us a screenshot of the post on WhatsApp or as an Instagram DM to @asnani_footwear, and we'll check availability for you.
